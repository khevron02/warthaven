I think this is the largest collection of Warthaven on the web, yet it is woefully incomplete.
If you have old 1980's or other strips, please sent them my way and I'll add them.


In my archiving of Oerthan Newsletters, I've collected these Warthavens.

-Baron Khevron, O.P.

These are from 1989 to 1997, all that I could find and/or scan.
I know he produced for over 15 years, weather monthly the entire time, I have no idea.
Any stories about Blackfox would be awesome to share here.
This is an effort to preserve and share the hilarity that is Warthaven in the SCA
(the Society for Critter Anachronuts).
- - I make no claims of ownership- -

Dates attributed to each may not be accurate, usually using the issue of the newsletter,
of which some contradicted others.
Most have a year associated. Titles are not always given either.
